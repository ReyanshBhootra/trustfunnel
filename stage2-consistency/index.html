Upload the PDF LinkedIn generates from your own profile. It is read in this browser and never uploaded anywhere.

How do I get this PDF? (3 steps, 20 seconds)
1. Open your own profile on linkedin.com and click More under your headline (on some accounts it sits under Resources).
2. Choose Save to PDF. LinkedIn downloads a file with your headline, experience and education.
3. Drop that file in the box above. We pull out the Experience section; you can review it before moving on.
