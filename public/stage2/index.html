Where do I get this PDF?
  1. Go to linkedin.com and open your own profile (click your photo, then "View profile").
  2. Under your headline, click More. On some accounts the button is called Resources.
  3. Choose Save to PDF. LinkedIn downloads a file with your headline, experience and education, usually named Profile.pdf.
  4. Drop that file in the box above. Nothing is uploaded: it is read here in your browser.
